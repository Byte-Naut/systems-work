# Working together

I take on bounded systems tasks through written, asynchronous collaboration. My current focus is WebAssembly runtime correctness, resource lifetimes, and host integration; closely related compiler, database, and runtime problems are also relevant.

A useful starting point is the repository and revision, the observed and expected behavior, the target environment, and a reproducer or acceptance tests. For a paid engagement, include the published participation rules, deliverables, review procedure, payment conditions, and whether the work is a one-off task or an ongoing role.

I provide a patch or experiment artifact, enough explanation to evaluate its behavior, and the applicable tests and limitations. Review and follow-up take place in writing. Required calls, live problem-solving sessions, and synchronous progress reports do not fit this arrangement.

I publish under **Byte-Naut**. Any administrative identity requirements need to be stated separately from public attribution and evaluated before engagement.
